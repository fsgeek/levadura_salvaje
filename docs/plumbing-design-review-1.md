# Review of plumbing design draft 1 (Codex, verbatim)

**Verdict: proceed after fixes.** ArangoDB is plausible; draft 1’s identities, evidence model, and acceptance contract need correction before slice 1.

1. **CRITICAL — Missing citations cannot be document→document edges.** [Schema, line 61](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:61). The v2 files contain absent sections/subdivisions, null paths (46 in 2025; 52 in 1997), and ranges (529/283). Resolutions may point through a repealed range element rather than an exact provision. Dropping these loses the central evidence; fabricating document vertices misrepresents it. **Fix:** give citation occurrences stable identities; store resolution assertions separately, keyed by occurrence, target snapshot, and resolver version. Preserve unresolved outcomes, range semantics, and zero/one/multiple target links. Audits should reference occurrences via `cite_index`.

2. **HIGH — `unit` conflates a numbered address with identity.** [Schema, line 49](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:49). CFR identity is `(volume_file, ordinal)`, not `sectno`. USC 119-4 has **57,176 rows but 57,161 distinct identifiers**; 119-110 has **58,388/58,371**. Even within a snapshot, identifiers collide. Citation paths also omit the provision files’ leading `s` and require dash normalization. **Fix:** immutable source-occurrence IDs, separate normalized address keys, explicit variant selection, and distinct relations for same-address, continuation, move, reuse, and restructure. Add provision containment for ancestor status and section rollups.

3. **HIGH — Pairing coverage and historical reuse are overstated.** [Acceptance #4](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:129). Turnover excludes repeated numbers: only **3,759/4,983** old rows remain eligible. Its `gone` means absent from the *unique-number map*, potentially including ambiguity. `in_1997` elsewhere means matching *any* old text hash, not matching the same numbered unit. All three loaded USC snapshots describe §683 as “Use of trust as an exchange fund”; they cannot demonstrate its older reuse. **Fix:** preserve these different pairing rules and unmatched/ambiguous statuses. Load the historical §683 evidence or use a supported reuse example. Record similarity pairing as a separate inference.

4. **HIGH — Snapshot dates are not validity intervals.** [Schema, lines 52–64](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:52). A target release identifies the resolution context; it does not establish when a legal rule applied. CFR volumes have mixed dates, and citations can explicitly concern the 1954 Code. **Fix:** distinguish ingestion/event time, source publication/current-through dates, resolution snapshot, and known legal applicability intervals. Permit unknown validity. Preserve volume membership and dates already represented in the Jabberwock mapping.

5. **HIGH — Mirroring ledger entries does not create drill-down membership.** [Measured edges](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:68). Relevant ledger entries contain summary values and hashed file references, not exhaustive per-item rows. External files have incompatible grains: section labels, citation audits, provision pairs, and turnover outcomes. Age/moved-citation rows omit occurrence IDs; `measured → document` cannot uniquely identify their evidence. **Fix:** define explicit import adapters and immutable result-row IDs, retain source file hash/line, and link each row to its actual subjects. Store metric-specific numerator/denominator membership rather than attaching every row to every observation derived from its file.

6. **HIGH — Findings #1–#3 exceed `aggregate(filter, group_by)`.** [Acceptance #1](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:123). They require distinct counts, joins, medians, audit weighting, and historical cohorts—not merely counts/rates over documents or references.

   - **#1:** obs-0138’s 61% is **3,211/5,222 citation occurrences**, although the prose sounds like distinct sections. Obs-0139 counts **69,823 resolved-path occurrences**; its file contains **69,875** total occurrences. The 2025 total includes unresolved ones.
   - **#2:** raw labels yield **1,500/1,675 = 89.6% current** and **771/1,675 = 46.0% silent**. “About 91%” and “two in five” are audit-adjusted estimates. There is no observed set of roughly 650 silent items to enumerate.
   - **#3:** requires move classifications and cross-file joins; **293/147** require the union of reuse/restructure exposures, with deduplication.

   **Fix:** versioned metric specifications with explicit grain, joins, exclusions, weights, rounding, and source snapshots. Either support a multi-metric query or accept one call per metric. Estimated quantities must drill to contributing observations and weights, not fictional item sets.

7. **HIGH — Acceptance can pass vacuously.** [Tests #1–#3](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:122). Returning cached ledger values satisfies numerical equality without computing anything. “Every loaded item” permits omitted populations; 20 ordinary samples miss unresolved citations, variants, and joins. Section-wide labels also have no supplied decisive text span. **Fix:** independently recompute metrics from hash-verified inputs; assert expected row counts, coverage, exclusions, and membership cardinalities. Test adversarial fixtures and reload idempotency. Distinguish whole-document evidence from annotated supporting spans.

8. **HIGH — Append-only corrections lack query semantics.** [Line 78](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:78). No correction relation, precedence, conflict handling, or effective-view rule is specified. Loading both extractor versions or repeated imports can double-count evidence while satisfying append-only storage. **Fix:** deterministic import identities, explicit supersession/withdrawal assertions, and queries pinned to a manifest plus correction policy. Preserve both historical and effective views.

9. **HIGH — Bounded pages can conceal population facts and invalidate citations.** [Tools](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:83). An aggregate cell has no defined persistent identity; continuation tokens have no snapshot or ordering contract. An expert can mistake 40 returned groups for the population, or drill a number after its membership changes. **Fix:** aggregate before pagination; return population totals, total groups, completeness/truncation status, grain, and denominator. Bind cell IDs and cursors to an immutable query specification and data manifest. Give `neighbors` direction and predicate filters.

10. **MEDIUM — Storage and footprint costs are unbudgeted.** [Open questions](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:138). The files imply roughly **320k per-snapshot resolution assertions** before measurement, history, and membership edges. USC `chars` totals are **86.95 million**, versus **29.03 million** at section level: nested provisions repeat text. The JSONLs contain hashes, not that text. A query examining 125k references has not shown an expert 125k items. **Fix:** benchmark complete storage/index costs; keep retrievable, hash-verified text with parser-versioned locators. Record query execution and returned pages separately from population membership. Store the query once, not on every visit edge.

11. **HIGH — Yanantin compatibility is asserted, not designed.** [Relation section](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:102). [Llika](/home/tony/projects/yanantin/docs/llika-spec.md:258) excludes **batch traversal**, not batch loading; it permits open-schema edge metadata and does not explicitly prohibit validity fields. Its updated boundary says customers own no graph primitives. The proposed membership, provenance, correction, footprint, and lineage machinery overlaps that ownership. The [Jabberwock mapping](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/jabberwock-mapping.md:8) already supplies observation/entity identities and dated edition membership. **Fix:** specify an adapter and ownership boundary: corpus ingestion/metrics here; reuse Yanantin identities and graph contracts where applicable. Map every new relation and provenance field, identify necessary extensions, and prove migration with a round-trip fixture. A shared database engine alone does not establish compatibility.
