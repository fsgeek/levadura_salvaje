# Review of plumbing design draft 2 (Codex, verbatim)

**Verdict: proceed after fixes.** Draft 2 improves the framing, but “takes every review-1 fix” overstates completion. I inspected the JSONL schemas, recomputed key counts, and verified 15 relevant result-file hashes against the ledger; all matched.

1. **HIGH — The pyramid lacks valid aggregation rules.** [Design:66–94](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:66). `{numerator, denominator, value}` cannot combine exact medians or distinct counts. Finding #3 demonstrates overlap: reuse/restructure exposures are **131 + 170 → 293**, while apparently healthy exposures are **78 + 71 → 147**. Summing children gives wrong answers. Arbitrary `filter` also cannot be answered from a fixed pyramid unless its dimensions were retained.  
   **Fix:** specify each metric’s merge state and permitted filters: distinct-ID sets/bitmaps, repeal-year histograms, weighted stratum totals. Otherwise recompute from immutable rows; explicitly distinguish exact results from approximations.

2. **HIGH — “Index, not store” breaks the promised reliable re-fetch unless storage has a retention contract.** [Design:21–36,154](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:21). Detecting a deleted source does not preserve evidence. Optional `sha256` also contradicts guaranteed hash verification. Existing CFR hashes cover **normalized text**, not ZIP/XML bytes; USC hashes cover flattened text excluding notes. Citation spans index that transformed text. USC JSONLs have neither unique XML selectors nor parent occurrence IDs.  
   **Fix:** require immutable artifact hashes, versioned extraction recipes, explicit raw/derived hash domains, encoding/span units, and variant-specific selectors. Separate stable identity from relocatable URI. Pin artifacts in an external archive or explicitly limit retention guarantees. Define missing-path versus hash-mismatch outcomes; moving a file usually establishes unreachability, not detectable relocation.

3. **HIGH — Declaring `result_row → occurrence` does not supply missing joins.** [Design:89–110,152](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:89). Age rows contain only `sectno,cited,section,repeal_year`; moved-citation rows contain `sectno,cited,now,now_exists`. Neither contains `volume_file,ordinal,cite_index`. Matching their available citation keys yields multiple candidates for **3,776/5,222 age rows** and **109/134 moved rows**. Ledger entries contain summaries and file references, not exhaustive membership.  
   **Fix:** specify adapters file by file. Regenerate occurrence-preserving outputs or replay the original extraction traversal with verified ordering and multiplicity. Preserve legacy row identities and reconstruction provenance. Explicitly include age, audit, moved-citation exposure and classified-reuse inputs; “per-section lens results” does not cover them.

4. **HIGH — Resolution assertions remain underspecified.** [Design:97–112](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:97). Snapshot-targeting edges preserve unresolved records, but there is no assertion entity tying together multiple targets, resolution basis and corrections. Citation ranges and statute range elements are different things. The source resolver also collapses duplicate paths using an “any in-force version” rule; no import rule maps that outcome back to particular variants. USC parent pointers cannot safely be inferred from colliding addresses.  
   **Fix:** reify each resolution with a stable ID, requested address/range, snapshot, resolver version, outcome and candidate-selection policy. Link separately to exact targets and supporting ancestors/range elements. Recover containment from source structure. Preserve `via`, `at_depth`, `missing_at_depth`, flags and range-expansion provenance.

5. **HIGH — Pairing coverage is still misstated, and absent pairs have no endpoint.** [Design:101–106](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:101). **3,759** is the number of uniquely numbered old sections, not cross-edition pairs. Actual counts are **3,217 paired + 542 absent**, with another **1,224 old occurrences excluded for repeated numbers**. An occurrence→occurrence edge cannot represent absence. Turnover’s 2,006 output rows cover fossil-related outcomes, not every pairing. `in_1997` remains an any-old-text-hash test, not continuity.  
   **Fix:** represent pairing assessments with optional targets and separate eligibility, ambiguity and absence statuses. Recompute coverage from both full citation files. Preserve exact-text membership separately from same-address pairing, continuation and reuse.

6. **HIGH — Findings #1–#3 still lack executable metric specifications or a single-call contract.** [Design:157–167](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:157). The draft lists three corrections, not complete specifications:
   - **#1:** needs distinct-address counts, section-level existential tests, audits, age distributions and turnover joins. **69,823 means non-null citation paths**, not successful resolutions.
   - **#2:** audit adjustment gives approximately **91.33% current** and **39.13% silent**. These require population stratum weights and exclusion of the extra AMT audit sample. They are derived estimates, not literal ledger values or enumerable estimated populations.
   - **#3:** **293/147** is reproducible by classification/exposure joins with deduplication; **134** requires deepest-moved-prefix logic. Pre-1997 reuse anecdotes need additional historical evidence or an explicit coverage limitation.  
   **Fix:** provide a claim-to-spec/source table with formulas, membership roles, exclusions and expected outputs. Define a multi-metric bundle call if “one call per finding” remains required; otherwise explicitly revise that requirement.

7. **HIGH — Correction semantics still stop at vocabulary.** [Design:55–60,111](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:55). Naming `supersedes`, `withdraws` and “effective” does not resolve competing corrections, cycles, replacement versus coexistence, or assertion selection. Including adapter version and locator in occurrence IDs can mint new subjects after an adapter upgrade or relocation.  
   **Fix:** separate source-occurrence identity from extraction/assertion identity; define correction scope, conflict handling and deterministic manifest selection. Publish manifests atomically with complete rollups so queries cannot mix generations.

8. **HIGH — Acceptance and paging can still pass with incorrect evidence.** [Design:164–185](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:164). Source row-count equality misses wrong joins; a membership count can be correct despite substituted members. “Correct” fixture answers and independent expected membership are unspecified. Truncation disclosure does not prevent duplicate/skipped cursor results; no stable ordering is required. Query records are referenced but not modeled.  
   **Fix:** pin explicit counts and expected IDs, including **194,868 citation occurrences / 319,861 snapshot outcomes**. Compare member sets and weighted contributions, not just counts. Test complete pagination against an unpaged oracle, including concurrent effective-manifest changes. Bind cursors to ordering, filters and correction policy; record returned IDs separately from queried population.

9. **MEDIUM — External text reduces duplication, but scale remains unbudgeted.** [Design:108–114,199–204](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:108). Membership across levels/specs/manifests can dominate the 319,861 resolution outcomes. Frozen manifests retain old pyramids. Repeated `follow` calls may repeatedly decompress and parse entire XML members. Footprints can still grow with every returned page/item.  
   **Fix:** benchmark a complete fixture including indexes, memberships, rollups and query records; state materialization and retention budgets. Use shared compressed memberships, partitioned external execution and verified extraction caches. Declare which fields `search` indexes and its coverage so missing hits are not mistaken for corpus absence.

10. **HIGH — Yanantin compatibility is deferred past the decisions that determine it.** [Design:138–148](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/plumbing-design.md:138). Separating `visited`/`lineage` collections does not satisfy Llika’s [customer boundary](/home/tony/projects/yanantin/docs/llika-spec.md:62). Containment, membership, provenance and correction primitives also overlap. “Llika’s edge shape” does not identify mappings to its four collections; corpus `member` even reverses Llika membership direction. The [Jabberwock mapping](/home/tony/projects/levadura_salvaje/docs/jabberwock-mapping.md:12) uses specific identity schemes and describes edition folds as unstored.  
    **Fix:** complete the ownership/relation/identity mapping and round-trip fixture before slice 1 fixes these contracts. Keep corpus metric caches explicitly distinct from authoritative Jabberwock observations and folds.

**Review-1 closure:** #4’s separation of times is substantively addressed. #3 drops the unsupported §683 acceptance example, but pairing remains incomplete. #1–2 and #5–10 are **partially addressed**, with gaps above. #11 removes the incorrect Llika exclusions but defers the requested compatibility design. None of those partial fixes should be marked closed yet.
