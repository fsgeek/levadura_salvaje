   seeds, not every alias (obs-0155).

   Both are ranges over how the readers' disagreements are counted, not confidence intervals.
   v3 is provisional until Codex's review is taken (docs/rule-currency-scorecard.md).
4. **The disagreement is the signal.** The readers agree about 90% of the time outside one
   section, and on 0 of 10 items inside it (1.909-6), with the direction flipping between two
   audits. Those items are rules with closed inputs and open-ended application. That's
   exploratory: the stratum was found by the disagreement it measures. v4 (`closed_inputs`,
   stamped, not run) is the confirmatory test. Naming other such rules before looking would
   test whether they form a class.
