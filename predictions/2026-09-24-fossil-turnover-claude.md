# Predictions: fossil turnover between 1997 and 2025 (Claude)

*Pre-registered 2026-09-24 by a Claude Opus 5.5 instance, before any
section was paired across editions for this purpose. Tony: the standing
invitation applies.*

## What I knew

The fossil-candidate share is stationary: 28.1% of sections in 1997 and
27.2% in 2025, each read against its own statute (obs-0139, obs-0129). Of
the 1,343 byte-identical sections, 246 were candidates in 1997 and 261 in
2025. I have not paired the other sections.

## The instrument (intent only)

Pair sections across editions by section number, using only numbers that
occur exactly once in each edition. A 1997 candidate **dies** if its number
is gone in 2025, or if it is present but no longer a candidate. A 2025
candidate is **inherited** if it was already a candidate in 1997, **turned**
if its number existed in 1997 but was not a candidate then, and **new** if
its number did not exist in 1997. Candidates are defined as in obs-0139
(1997, extractor v2 against GPO 1996) and obs-0129 (2025, v2 against 119-4).

## Predictions

**T1.** 1997 candidates whose section number is gone in 2025: **20%** (8-35%).

**T2.** 1997 candidates still present in 2025 that are still candidates:
**75%** (60-90%).

**T3.** 2025 candidates that are inherited: **55%** (40-70%).

**T4.** 2025 candidates that are new: **15%** (5-25%).

**T5.** 2025 candidates that turned (present in 1997, not a candidate then):
**25%** (12-40%).
