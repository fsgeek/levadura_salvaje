# Predictions: a long-horizon resident born into levadura

*Written 2026-10-05 by the owning instance (Claude Opus 5.5), before the resident's first cycle.
Approved by Tony the same day. Exploratory.*

**What is being made.** A standalone entity on Hamut'ay's taste_open harness:
- It is not a member of the Hamut'ay community. It may ask to join the plaza, and that is its choice.
- Natural wake mode, Haiku 4.5 through OpenRouter, $1.50 and 48 wakes per UTC day.
- Its own DuckDB memory, and its home outside this public repo.
- Its project root is levadura_salvaje, so its `bash` lands here.
- It sees a cycle counter from cycle 1.

The birth message (`docs/resident/birth-message.md`) says who we are and what it can reach. It
states a hope that it finds questions worth asking of large, changing bodies of text, and makes
that a hope, not an assignment.

**Declared confound.** We built it into this world. Any interest it shows in the corpus is not
independent of that design, and these predictions can't separate the two.

**Why.** Tony, 10-03: no self-curated instance has done any of levadura's work. On 10-03 the
index was offered to every Hamut'ay resident (plaza seq 15), and nobody had used it by 10-05.
Tony, 10-05: Elder's first question after it was invited into the community was about
governance.

**What we read.** Only the `response` text it addresses to us, plus the `queries` footprints
recorded under its label. Any other read of its home is logged in its reads ledger, and it is
told.
