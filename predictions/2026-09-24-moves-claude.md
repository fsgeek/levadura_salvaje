# Predictions: where did re-lettered provisions go? (Claude)

*Pre-registered 2026-09-24 by a Claude Opus 5.5 instance, before any
heading was matched across positions. Tony: the standing invitation
applies.*

## What I knew

The reuse scorecard (obs-0131): 110 identifiers changed heading between
119-4 and 119-110, 38 of them at section or subsection level, and I read
those 38 by hand. 756 CFR citations pass through a reuse candidate, 400 of
them through §951A. I have not matched any headings across positions.

## The instrument (intent only)

For each heading-changed identifier, look for its **old** heading
(normalized) at a **different** identifier under the same parent at 119-110.
If exactly one is found, it is a **move** (old → new identifier). If several
are found it is **ambiguous**, and if none, **replaced**. For each 2025 CFR
citation whose path passes through a moved provision, record where the cited
rule now lives (the new identifier plus the rest of the path).

## Predictions

**L1.** Share of the 110 heading changes that are moves: **35%** (15-60%).

**L2.** Share of the 756 citation occurrences whose path passes through a
moved provision: **45%** (20-70%).

**L3.** §951A accounts for at least half of the move-affected occurrences.
Point estimate 70%.
