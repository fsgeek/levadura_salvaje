# Predictions: how long have the regulations' dead citations been dead? (Claude)

*Pre-registered 2026-09-24 by a Claude Opus 5.5 instance, before any repeal
date was tallied against citations. Tony: the standing invitation applies.*

## What I knew

The fossil scorecard: 5,194 v1 citation occurrences (v2 similar) resolve as
repealed at 119-4. Heavy hitters are §902, §1201, §§810/815, §71 (2017),
§936 (2018), §809 (2004), §1251 (1984) and §615 (1976). To check the format
I confirmed that every repealed *section* element's heading carries a
dated public-law citation, and that 25 repealed subdivisions carry none. I
have not tallied dates against citations.

## The instrument (intent only)

For each 2025 CFR citation occurrence (extractor v2) whose **section** is
repealed at 119-4 (directly or through a repealed range), the repeal year is
the latest date in that section element's heading. Occurrences through
repealed subdivisions of in-force sections are excluded, because their
elements carry no date. Also: per distinct cited repealed section.

## Predictions

**A1.** Median repeal year over citation occurrences: **1990** (1980-2005).

**A2.** Share of occurrences repealed at least 20 years before 2025
(repeal year ≤ 2005): **60%** (40-80%).

**A3.** Share of occurrences repealed by the 2017 tax act (Pub. L. 115-97,
year 2017): **20%** (8-35%).

**A4.** Among distinct cited repealed sections, the most common repeal
decade is the **1970s** (fails otherwise). The Tax Reform Act of 1976 swept
out a great deal of deadwood.
